**2. Why the Optimum Is Here**

Two opposing forces set the ceiling at 85.7 units:

- **Shipping more boosts revenue, but with diminishing returns.** The market saturates (saturation_factor = 0.02): each additional unit you ship earns a little less than the last, because you're filling demand that's harder and harder to reach.
- **Shipping more also inflates costs non-linearly.** Congestion (congestion_factor = 0.05) means that as volume rises, trucks slow down, routes crowd, and cost-per-unit climbs. At high volumes this congestion cost grows faster than revenue.

At 85.7 units, the marginal revenue from one more unit exactly equals the marginal cost of delivering it. Push past that and you're spending more to ship than you're earning.
