### Why 85.7 units is the sweet spot

Two forces pull in opposite directions:

- **Shipping more earns more revenue**, but each additional unit earns less (market saturation — customers are already being served, new orders are harder to fill at full price). Revenue per unit falls from $20 at low volumes toward ~$7 at 85 units.
- **Shipping more costs more** — not just proportionally, but exponentially. Congestion (traffic, dock capacity, driver overtime) adds $0.05 per unit *per unit shipped*, so shipping twice as many units more than doubles congestion costs.

At 85.7 units, the marginal revenue from one more shipment exactly equals the marginal cost of congestion. Beyond that point, every extra unit *destroys* profit.
