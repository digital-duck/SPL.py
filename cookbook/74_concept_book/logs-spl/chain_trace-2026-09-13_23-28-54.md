## Atom
Picture this: everything you see—the chair you sit on, the apple you ate, even you!—is made up of stuff. But what *is* that "stuff"? It's so incredibly small that we need a special name for it: an atom. Imagine matter is like a giant collection of LEGO bricks. The atom is the smallest possible brick; if you break it into smaller pieces, it stops being that specific kind of material.

So, what *is* inside this tiny building block? Atoms aren't solid little spheres! They have a central core called the nucleus, and zooming around that core are super-fast particles we call electrons. Think of the nucleus as the sun at the center of a solar system, and the orbiting electrons are the planets spinning far away.

While they look simple, atoms actually come in different flavors (like oxygen atoms or gold atoms). These "flavors" tell us how many protons—the positively charged particles stuck in the nucleus—it has. This number is what defines the element!
