## France 1848 February Revolution Second Republic

The February Revolution of 1848 represents a profound historical process characterized by the rapid systemic failure of the July Monarchy regime under King Louis-Philippe I. Defined as a popular uprising fueled by economic distress and political disenfranchisement, the revolution rapidly dismantled the existing constitutional order. The core mechanism was a confluence of popular protest (specifically related to bread prices and working-class demands) that transitioned into organized political action, leading to the military's refusal to fire upon protestors. This collapse necessitated the declaration of the Second Republic, representing an immediate shift in sovereignty from monarchical legitimacy to a provisional republican authority
