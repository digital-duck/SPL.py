# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A furniture workshop produces chairs and tables. Each chair requires 2 hours of labor and 3 kg of wood, earning $20 profit. Each table requires 4 hours of labor and 5 kg of wood, earning $30 profit. The workshop has 24 hours of labor and 30 kg of wood available per day. Both quantities must be non-negative. Maximize daily profit.

**Solver status:** `Optimal`
**Optimal objective value:** 200

**Decision variables:**
  - chairs = 10
  - tables = 0

## Interpretation

The optimal plan is to produce **10 chairs and 0 tables** per day, yielding a maximum daily profit of **$200**.

The **wood supply is binding**: all 30 kg are consumed by the 10 chairs. Labor, however, is not fully used — only 20 of the 24 available hours are needed, leaving 4 hours idle.

The intuition is straightforward: chairs earn $6.67 of profit per kilogram of wood versus $6.00 for tables, so when wood is the scarce resource, the workshop extracts more value by dedicating it entirely to chairs.

**Recommendation**: Focus daily production on chairs; the 4 idle labor hours represent an opportunity to expand wood supply rather than hire more workers.
