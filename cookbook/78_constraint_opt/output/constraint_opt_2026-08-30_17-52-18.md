## Interpretation

The factory should produce **10 Armchairs, 22.5 Coffee Tables, and 8 Office Desks** daily, leaving all other product lines idle. This yields a **maximum daily profit of $1,470**.

Three constraints are fully binding: **lumber** (all 150 kg used), **upholstery fabric** (all 15 m² used), and **steel** (all 12 kg used). Labor has 17 hours of unused capacity, meaning hiring more workers would not increase profit — the binding bottlenecks are materials.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize procurement of lumber, fabric, and steel; additional labor capacity is already in surplus and cannot be leveraged without more raw materials.
