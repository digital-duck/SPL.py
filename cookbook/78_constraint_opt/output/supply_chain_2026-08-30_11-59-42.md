# Supply Chain Optimization Report

**Problem:** A company has two warehouses and three retail stores. Warehouse W1 has 80 units of inventory; Warehouse W2 has 60 units. Store S1 needs 50 units, Store S2 needs 40 units, Store S3 needs 50 units. Shipping costs per unit: W1 to S1 costs $2, W1 to S2 costs $3, W1 to S3 costs $1; W2 to S1 costs $5, W2 to S2 costs $4, W2 to S3 costs $8. All store demand must be met exactly. Minimize total shipping cost.

## Solution

Here's the solution to the transportation problem, working through each stage of the Minimum Cost Method:

**1. Cost Matrix and Supply/Demand Table:**

First, we organize the given information into a table. The cost matrix represents the cost per unit shipped from each warehouse to each store:

|        | S1   | S2   | S3   |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **W1** | $2   | $3   | $1   |
| **W2** | $5   | $4   | $8   |



Next, we create a supply/demand table summarizing the inventory levels and demand requirements:

|        | W1    | W2    | Total |
|--------|-------|-------|-------|
| S1     | 80    | 0     | 50    |
| S2     | 0     | 60    | 40    |
| S3     | 0     | 0     | 50    |
| **Total** | 80+60=140 | 60   | 140  |


**2. Applying the Minimum Cost Method:**

We begin with the Northwest Corner (origin warehouse W1, first store S1).

* **Iteration 1:**
    * Ship 50 units from W1 to S1: This satisfies the demand for S1 (80-50=30 remaining inventory at W1, and 50 units shipped) and the demand for S1 (50-50 = 0 remaining).  Cost: 50 * $2 = $100
    * Remaining Supply at W1: 30 units
    * Remaining Demand at S1: 0 units

Now, we move to the Southeast Corner (last warehouse W2, last store S3).

* **Iteration 2:**
    * Ship 40 units from W2 to S3: This satisfies the demand for S3 (50-40 = 10 remaining inventory at W2) and the demand for S3 (50 - 40  = 10 units shipped). Cost: 40 * $8 = $320.
    * Remaining Supply at W2: 20 units
    * Remaining Demand at S3: 10 units


Now, we move to the Northeast Corner (W1, S2)

* **Iteration 3:**
   * Ship 30 units from W1 to S2:  This satisfies the demand for S2 (40 - 30 = 10 remaining inventory at W2), the demand for S2 and uses up all remaining inventory at W1. Cost: 30 * $3 = $90
   * Remaining Supply at W1: 0 units
   * Remaining Demand at S2: 10 units

Now we move to the Southwest Corner (W2, S1)

* **Iteration 4:**
    * Ship 10 Units from W2 to S1: This satisfies demand for S1 and utilizes remaining inventory at W2. Cost: 10 * $5 = $50
    * Remaining Supply at W2: 0 units
    * Remaining Demand at S1: 10 units


Final Solution:

|        | S1   | S2   | S3   |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **W1** | 50   | 30  | 0  |
| **W2** | 0   | 10  | 40  |



**3. Optimal Shipping Plan and Total Minimum Cost:**

* W1 ships 50 units to S1, 30 units to S2, and 0 units to S3.
* W2 ships 0 units to S1, 10 units to S2, and 40 units to S3.

Total minimum cost: (50 * $2) + (30 * $3) + (0 * $1) + (0 * $5) + (10 * $4) + (40 * $8) = $100 + $90 + $0 + $0 + $40 + $320 = $550
