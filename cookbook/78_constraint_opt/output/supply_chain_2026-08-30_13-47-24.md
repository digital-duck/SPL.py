# Supply Chain Optimization Report

**Problem:** A regional distributor has 4 warehouses and 5 retail stores. Warehouse W1 holds 90 units, W2 holds 70 units, W3 holds 80 units, W4 holds 60 units (total supply 300). Store S1 needs 60 units, S2 needs 55 units, S3 needs 70 units, S4 needs 50 units, S5 needs 65 units (total demand 300). Shipping cost per unit: W1 to S1 $3, W1 to S2 $2, W1 to S3 $5, W1 to S4 $8, W1 to S5 $4. W2 to S1 $6, W2 to S2 $4, W2 to S3 $3, W2 to S4 $5, W2 to S5 $7. W3 to S1 $4, W3 to S2 $7, W3 to S3 $2, W3 to S4 $6, W3 to S5 $3. W4 to S1 $8, W4 to S2 $5, W4 to S3 $6, W4 to S4 $2, W4 to S5 $9. All store demand must be exactly met. Minimize total shipping cost.
