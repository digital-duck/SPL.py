# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A furniture workshop produces chairs and tables. Each chair requires 2 hours of labor and 3 kg of wood, earning $20 profit. Each table requires 4 hours of labor and 5 kg of wood, earning $30 profit. The workshop has 24 hours of labor and 30 kg of wood available per day. Both quantities must be non-negative. Maximize daily profit.

**Solver status:** `Optimal`
**Optimal objective value:** 200

**Decision variables:**
  - chairs = 10
  - tables = 0

## Interpretation

The solver recommends producing **10 chairs and 0 tables** per day. This yields the maximum achievable daily profit of **$200**. The wood supply (30 kg) is fully consumed — it is the binding constraint that caps output. The labor supply (24 hours) is not fully used; 4 hours remain idle. Although tables earn $10 more per unit than chairs, they also consume more wood per unit, making chairs the more efficient use of the scarce resource. **Recommendation: run the workshop at full wood capacity producing chairs only, and explore whether additional wood supply can be sourced to unlock further profit.**

## Solution Verification

**Given:** chairs = 10, tables = 0

**Labor constraint** (2 × chairs + 4 × tables ≤ 24):
2 × 10 + 4 × 0 = 20 + 0 = **20 ≤ 24** ✓ — satisfied, **slack = 4 hours** (not binding)

**Wood constraint** (3 × chairs + 5 × tables ≤ 30):
3 × 10 + 5 × 0 = 30 + 0 = **30 ≤ 30** ✓ — satisfied, **slack = 0** (binding — tight)

**Non-negativity:**
chairs = 10 ≥ 0 ✓ | tables = 0 ≥ 0 ✓

**Objective value** (20 × chairs + 30 × tables):
20 × 10 + 30 × 0 = **200 + 0 = $200**

The computed objective of **$200 matches the solver's reported value exactly.** The solution is verified correct.
