# Supply Chain Optimization Report

**Problem:** A company has two warehouses and three retail stores. Warehouse W1 has 80 units of inventory; Warehouse W2 has 60 units. Store S1 needs 50 units, Store S2 needs 40 units, Store S3 needs 50 units. Shipping costs per unit: W1 to S1 costs $2, W1 to S2 costs $3, W1 to S3 costs $1; W2 to S1 costs $5, W2 to S2 costs $4, W2 to S3 costs $8. All store demand must be met exactly. Minimize total shipping cost.

## Solution

**1. Cost Matrix and Supply/Demand Table:**

First, we represent the shipping costs in a cost matrix. This matrix will have dimensions (number of supply points x number of demand points). In this case, it’s a 2x3 matrix:

|         | S1    | S2    | S3    |
| :------ | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **W1**  | $2    | $3    | $1    |
| **W2**  | $5    | $4    | $8    |

Next, we create a table to represent the supply and demand constraints.

| Warehouse | W1    | W2    | Total Supply |
| :-------- | :---- | :---- | :------------ |
| Demand S1 | 50    | 0     | 50            |
| Demand S2 | 0     | 40    | 40            |
| Demand S3 | 50    | 0     | 50            |
| **Total** | 80    | 60    | 180           |


**2. Applying the Minimum Cost Method:**

The Minimum Cost Method involves repeatedly shipping from the source with the lowest cost to the destination with the highest cost, until all supply and demand are satisfied.

* **Iteration 1:**
   - S1 needs 50 units and W1 has $1 per unit to S3.  S2 needs 40 and W2 has $4 per unit to S2 is not a good choice as it's higher than W1’s cost to S3. Therefore, we ship 50 units from W1 to S1 at $1/unit = 50 * $1 = $50.
   - Remaining supply at W1: 80 - 50 = 30 units.
   - Remaining demand at S1: 50 - 50 = 0 units.
   - Remaining supply at W2: 60 units.
   - Remaining demand at S2: 40 units.
   - Remaining supply at W2: 60 units.
   - Remaining demand at S3: 50 units
   - New Cost Matrix (after shipping 50 from W1 to S1):

     |         | S1    | S2    | S3    |
     | :------ | :---- | :---- | :---- |
     | **W1**  | $2    | $3    | $1    |
     | **W2**  | $5    | $4    | $8    |


* **Iteration 2:**

   - S2 needs 40 units and W2 has $4 per unit to S2. We ship 40 units from W2 to S2 at $4/unit = 40 * $4 = $160
   - Remaining supply at W2: 60 - 40 = 20 units.
    - New Cost Matrix (after shipping 40 from W2 to S2):

     |         | S1    | S2    | S3    |
     | :------ | :---- | :---- | :---- |
     | **W1**  | $2    | $3    | $1    |
     | **W2**  | $5    | $4    | $8    |

* **Iteration 3:**
   - S3 needs 50 units and W1 has $1 per unit to S3. We ship 50 units from W1 to S3 at $1/unit = 50 * $1 = $50
   - Remaining supply at W1: 30 units.
   - Remaining demand at S3: 50 - 50 = 0 units.
    - New Cost Matrix (after shipping 50 from W1 to S3):

     |         | S1    | S2    | S3    |
     | :------ | :---- | :---- | :---- |
     | **W1**  | $2    | $3    | $1    |
     | **W2**  | $5    | $4    | $8    |


* **Iteration 4:**

   - W1 has no supply left and S1 needs nothing.
   - W2 has 20 units remaining and S1 needs nothing.
   - W2 has no supply left and S2 needs nothing.
