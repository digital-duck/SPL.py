# Supply Chain Optimization Report

**Problem:** A company has two warehouses and three retail stores. Warehouse W1 has 80 units of inventory; Warehouse W2 has 60 units. Store S1 needs 50 units, Store S2 needs 40 units, Store S3 needs 50 units. Shipping costs per unit: W1 to S1 costs $2, W1 to S2 costs $3, W1 to S3 costs $1; W2 to S1 costs $5, W2 to S2 costs $4, W2 to S3 costs $8. All store demand must be met exactly. Minimize total shipping cost.

## Solution

### Step 1 — Cost Matrix and Supply/Demand Table

```
          S1    S2    S3   | Supply
W1        $2    $3    $1   |  80
W2        $5    $4    $8   |  60
──────────────────────────────────
Demand    50    40    50   | 140
```

Total supply = 140, total demand = 140 → balanced problem. No dummy row/column needed.

---

### Step 2 — Minimum Cost Method (initial feasible solution)

Rank all six cells by unit cost:

| Rank | Route  | Cost |
|------|--------|------|
| 1    | W1→S3  | $1   |
| 2    | W1→S1  | $2   |
| 3    | W1→S2  | $3   |
| 4    | W2→S2  | $4   |
| 5    | W2→S1  | $5   |
| 6    | W2→S3  | $8   |

**Allocation 1 — W1→S3 ($1):** min(W1 remaining=80, S3 demand=50) = **50 units**
- W1 remaining: 80 − 50 = 30
- S3 satisfied; remove S3 column.

**Allocation 2 — W1→S1 ($2):** min(30, 50) = **30 units**
- W1 remaining: 30 − 30 = 0
- S1 remaining: 50 − 30 = 20; remove W1 row.

**Allocation 3 — W2→S2 ($4):** min(60, 40) = **40 units** (W1→S2 is gone; next cheapest available is W2→S2)
- W2 remaining: 60 − 40 = 20
- S2 satisfied; remove S2 column.

**Allocation 4 — W2→S1 ($5):** only cell remaining; min(20, 20) = **20 units**
- W2 remaining: 0; S1 satisfied.

**Initial basic feasible solution:**

| Route  | Units |
|--------|-------|
| W1→S1  | 30    |
| W1→S3  | 50    |
| W2→S1  | 20    |
| W2→S2  | 40    |

Initial cost = 30×2 + 50×1 + 20×5 + 40×4 = 60 + 50 + 100 + 160 = **$370**

---

### Step 3 — MODI Optimality Check (u–v method)

A 2×3 problem requires m+n−1 = 4 basic variables; we have exactly 4, so no degeneracy.

**Set u₁ = 0** and solve u_i + v_j = c_ij for each basic cell:

| Basic cell | Equation            | Result |
|------------|---------------------|--------|
| W1→S1      | u₁ + v₁ = 2        | v₁ = 2 |
| W1→S3      | u₁ + v₃ = 1        | v₃ = 1 |
| W2→S1      | u₂ + v₁ = 5        | u₂ = 3 |
| W2→S2      | u₂ + v₂ = 4        | v₂ = 1 |

**Reduced costs for non-basic cells** (Δ_ij = c_ij − u_i − v_j):

- W1→S2: 3 − 0 − 1 = **+2** (no improvement possible)
- W2→S3: 8 − 3 − 1 = **+4** (no improvement possible)

Both reduced costs are strictly positive → **no entering variable exists → current solution is optimal.**

### Optimal Shipping Plan

| Route  | Units | Unit Cost |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| W1→S1  | 30    | $2        |
| W1→S3  | 50    | $1        |
| W2→S1  | 20    | $5        |
| W2→S2  | 40    | $4        |

**Minimum total cost = $370**

## Verification

### Supply constraints

| Warehouse | Shipments        | Arithmetic      | Supply | Status  |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|--------|---------|
| W1        | W1→S1 + W1→S3   | 30 + 50 = 80    | 80     | Binding |
| W2        | W2→S1 + W2→S2   | 20 + 40 = 60    | 60     | Binding |

Both warehouses are fully shipped; zero slack.

### Demand constraints

| Store | Receipts           | Arithmetic      | Demand | Met?    |
|-------|--------------------|-----------------|--------|---------|
| S1    | W1→S1 + W2→S1     | 30 + 20 = 50    | 50     | Exactly |
| S2    | W2→S2              | 40 = 40         | 40     | Exactly |
| S3    | W1→S3              | 50 = 50         | 50     | Exactly |

All store demands are met exactly.

### Total cost (step by step)

```
W1→S1:  30 × $2 = $ 60
W1→S3:  50 × $1 = $ 50
W2→S1:  20 × $5 = $100
W2→S2:  40 × $4 = $160
                 ──────
Total           = $370
```

The minimum total shipping cost is **$370**.
