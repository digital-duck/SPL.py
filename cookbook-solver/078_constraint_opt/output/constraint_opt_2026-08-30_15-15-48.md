# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A furniture factory produces 10 product lines. Each unit requires labor (hours), lumber (kg), upholstery fabric (m²), and steel (kg), and earns a fixed profit. Product specifications per unit: Dining Chair needs 2.0h labor, 3.0kg lumber, 0.8m² fabric, 0.0kg steel, profit $25. Dining Table needs 4.0h labor, 8.0kg lumber, 0.0m² fabric, 0.0kg steel, profit $55. Office Desk needs 3.5h labor, 5.0kg lumber, 0.0m² fabric, 1.5kg steel, profit $45. Bookcase needs 5.0h labor, 9.0kg lumber, 0.0m² fabric, 0.5kg steel, profit $58. Wardrobe needs 7.0h labor, 14.0kg lumber, 0.0m² fabric, 0.5kg steel, profit $85. Armchair needs 3.0h labor, 2.0kg lumber, 1.5m² fabric, 0.0kg steel, profit $48. Bed Frame needs 5.0h labor, 10.0kg lumber, 0.0m² fabric, 2.0kg steel, profit $72. Coffee Table needs 2.0h labor, 4.0kg lumber, 0.0m² fabric, 0.0kg steel, profit $28. Storage Cabinet needs 4.0h labor, 7.0kg lumber, 0.0m² fabric, 1.0kg steel, profit $52. Lounge Sofa needs 4.0h labor, 3.0kg lumber, 2.5m² fabric, 0.0kg steel, profit $68. Available daily: 120 labor hours, 150 kg lumber, 15 m² upholstery fabric, 12 kg steel. Production quantities are continuous (fractional units allowed). Maximize total daily profit.

## Solution

### 1. Decision Variables, Objective, and Constraints

Let $x_1, \ldots, x_{10}$ denote daily production quantities of Dining Chair, Dining Table, Office Desk, Bookcase, Wardrobe, Armchair, Bed Frame, Coffee Table, Storage Cabinet, and Lounge Sofa respectively. All $x_i \ge 0$.

**Objective:** Maximize $Z = 25x_1 + 55x_2 + 45x_3 + 58x_4 + 85x_5 + 48x_6 + 72x_7 + 28x_8 + 52x_9 + 68x_{10}$

**Constraints (four resources):**

| Resource | Constraint | RHS |
|---|---|---|
| Labor (h) | $2x_1 + 4x_2 + 3.5x_3 + 5x_4 + 7x_5 + 3x_6 + 5x_7 + 2x_8 + 4x_9 + 4x_{10}$ | $\le 120$ |
| Lumber (kg) | $3x_1 + 8x_2 + 5x_3 + 9x_4 + 14x_5 + 2x_6 + 10x_7 + 4x_8 + 7x_9 + 3x_{10}$ | $\le 150$ |
| Fabric (m²) | $0.8x_1 + 1.5x_6 + 2.5x_{10}$ | $\le 15$ |
| Steel (kg) | $1.5x_3 + 0.5x_4 + 0.5x_5 + 2x_7 + x_9$ | $\le 12$ |

### 2. Finding the Optimal Vertex

With 10 variables and 4 constraints, the optimal basic feasible solution (BFS) has at most 4 positive variables. I identify the optimal vertex by profiling efficiency and applying simplex pivots.

**Profit per kg of lumber** (all products consume lumber; it will likely bind):

| Product | Profit/lumber ($/kg) | Secondary constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Armchair | 48 ÷ 2 = **24.00** | fabric: 1.5 m²/unit |
| Lounge Sofa | 68 ÷ 3 = 22.67 | fabric: 2.5 m²/unit |
| Office Desk | 45 ÷ 5 = 9.00 | steel: 1.5 kg/unit |
| Dining Chair | 25 ÷ 3 = 8.33 | fabric: 0.8 m²/unit |
| Storage Cabinet | 52 ÷ 7 = 7.43 | steel: 1.0 kg/unit |
| Coffee Table | 28 ÷ 4 = 7.00 | none |
| Bed Frame | 72 ÷ 10 = 7.20 | steel: 2.0 kg/unit |
| Dining Table | 55 ÷ 8 = 6.875 | — |
| Bookcase | 58 ÷ 9 = 6.44 | steel: 0.5 kg/unit |
| Wardrobe | 85 ÷ 14 = 6.07 | — |

**Step A — Allocate fabric to highest lumber-efficiency product (Armchair).**  
Fabric constraint binds: $1.5 x_6 = 15 \Rightarrow x_6 = 10$.  
Uses: $10 \times 3 = 30$ h labor, $10 \times 2 = 20$ kg lumber.  
Remaining: labor 90 h, lumber 130 kg, steel 12 kg (fabric exhausted).

**Step B — Allocate steel to the highest lumber-efficiency steel product (Office Desk at 9.00 $/kg lumber).**  
Steel constraint binds: $1.5 x_3 = 12 \Rightarrow x_3 = 8$.  
Uses: $8 \times 3.5 = 28$ h labor, $8 \times 5 = 40$ kg lumber.  
Remaining: labor 62 h, lumber 90 kg (steel exhausted, no fabric).

**Step C — Fill remaining lumber with best no-steel no-fabric product (Coffee Table at 7.00 $/kg).**  
Lumber constraint binds: $4 x_8 = 90 \Rightarrow x_8 = 22.5$.  
Uses: $22.5 \times 2 = 45$ h labor, $22.5 \times 4 = 90$ kg lumber.  
Remaining: labor 17 h (slack).

**Candidate vertex:** $x_3 = 8$, $x_6 = 10$, $x_8 = 22.5$; all other $x_i = 0$.

### 3. Certifying Optimality via Dual Prices

Three resource constraints bind (lumber, fabric, steel); labor is slack $\Rightarrow$ its shadow price $\pi_W = 0$.

Solve the three-equation dual system using the three basic variables:

$$\text{Coffee Table: } 28 = 4\pi_L \Rightarrow \pi_L = 7.00 \text{ \$/kg lumber}$$

$$\text{Armchair: } 48 = 2(7) + 1.5\pi_F \Rightarrow 1.5\pi_F = 34 \Rightarrow \pi_F = \tfrac{68}{3} \approx 22.67 \text{ \$/m}^2$$

$$\text{Office Desk: } 45 = 5(7) + 1.5\pi_S \Rightarrow 1.5\pi_S = 10 \Rightarrow \pi_S = \tfrac{20}{3} \approx 6.67 \text{ \$/kg steel}$$

**Reduced cost check** (must be $\le 0$ for all non-basic variables):

| Non-basic variable | $c_j$ | $7 \times \text{lbr}$ | $\tfrac{68}{3} \times \text{fab}$ | $\tfrac{20}{3} \times \text{stl}$ | Reduced cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dining Chair ($x_1$) | 25 | $7 \times 3 = 21$ | $\tfrac{68}{3} \times 0.8 = \tfrac{272}{15} \approx 18.13$ | 0 | $25 - 39.13 = \mathbf{-14.13}$ |
| Dining Table ($x_2$) | 55 | $7 \times 8 = 56$ | 0 | 0 | $55 - 56 = \mathbf{-1.00}$ |
| Bookcase ($x_4$) | 58 | $7 \times 9 = 63$ | 0 | $\tfrac{20}{3} \times 0.5 = \tfrac{10}{3} \approx 3.33$ | $58 - 66.33 = \mathbf{-8.33}$ |
| Wardrobe ($x_5$) | 85 | $7 \times 14 = 98$ | 0 | $\tfrac{10}{3} \approx 3.33$ | $85 - 101.33 = \mathbf{-16.33}$ |
| Bed Frame ($x_7$) | 72 | $7 \times 10 = 70$ | 0 | $\tfrac{20}{3} \times 2 = \tfrac{40}{3} \approx 13.33$ | $72 - 83.33 = \mathbf{-11.33}$ |
| Storage Cabinet ($x_9$) | 52 | $7 \times 7 = 49$ | 0 | $\tfrac{20}{3} \approx 6.67$ | $52 - 55.67 = \mathbf{-3.67}$ |
| Lounge Sofa ($x_{10}$) | 68 | $7 \times 3 = 21$ | $\tfrac{68}{3} \times 2.5 = \tfrac{170}{3} \approx 56.67$ | 0 | $68 - 77.67 = \mathbf{-9.67}$ |

Every reduced cost is strictly negative. By LP optimality conditions, no pivot can improve the objective. The vertex is **globally optimal**.

## Verification

**Plug in** $x_3 = 8$, $x_6 = 10$, $x_8 = 22.5$, all others $= 0$.

**Labor:**
$$8 \times 3.5 + 10 \times 3 + 22.5 \times 2 = 28 + 30 + 45 = 103 \le 120 \quad \textbf{slack } (17 \text{ h})$$

**Lumber:**
$$8 \times 5 + 10 \times 2 + 22.5 \times 4 = 40 + 20 + 90 = 150 \le 150 \quad \textbf{binding}$$

**Fabric:**
$$8 \times 0 + 10 \times 1.5 + 22.5 \times 0 = 0 + 15 + 0 = 15 \le 15 \quad \textbf{binding}$$

**Steel:**
$$8 \times 1.5 + 10 \times 0 + 22.5 \times 0 = 12 + 0 + 0 = 12 \le 12 \quad \textbf{binding}$$

**Objective (step by step):**
$$8 \times \$45 = \$360$$
$$10 \times \$48 = \$480$$
$$22.5 \times \$28 = \$630$$
$$\$360 + \$480 + \$630 = \boxed{\$1{,}470}$$

All constraints satisfied; three bind (lumber, fabric, steel); one is slack (labor). Objective confirmed at **$1,470/day**.
