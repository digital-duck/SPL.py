# Supply Chain Optimization Report

**Problem:** A regional distributor has 4 warehouses and 5 retail stores. Warehouse W1 holds 90 units, W2 holds 70 units, W3 holds 80 units, W4 holds 60 units (total supply 300). Store S1 needs 60 units, S2 needs 55 units, S3 needs 70 units, S4 needs 50 units, S5 needs 65 units (total demand 300). Shipping cost per unit: W1 to S1 $3, W1 to S2 $2, W1 to S3 $5, W1 to S4 $8, W1 to S5 $4. W2 to S1 $6, W2 to S2 $4, W2 to S3 $3, W2 to S4 $5, W2 to S5 $7. W3 to S1 $4, W3 to S2 $7, W3 to S3 $2, W3 to S4 $6, W3 to S5 $3. W4 to S1 $8, W4 to S2 $5, W4 to S3 $6, W4 to S4 $2, W4 to S5 $9. All store demand must be exactly met. Minimize total shipping cost.

## Solution

### Step 1 — Cost Matrix and Supply/Demand Table

|        |  S1  |  S2  |  S3  |  S4  |  S5  | Supply |
|--------|------|------|------|------|------|--------|
| **W1** |  $3  |  $2  |  $5  |  $8  |  $4  |   90   |
| **W2** |  $6  |  $4  |  $3  |  $5  |  $7  |   70   |
| **W3** |  $4  |  $7  |  $2  |  $6  |  $3  |   80   |
| **W4** |  $8  |  $5  |  $6  |  $2  |  $9  |   60   |
| **Demand** | 60 | 55 | 70 | 50 | 65 | **300** |

Total supply = 90+70+80+60 = 300 = Total demand. The problem is balanced.  
A 4×5 problem requires m+n−1 = **8 basic variables** in any non-degenerate BFS.

### Step 2 — Initial BFS via Minimum Cost Method

Sort all 20 cells by cost and allocate greedily, crossing out exhausted rows/columns.

| Round | Cell   | Cost | Alloc | Reasoning |
|-------|--------|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | W3→S3 | $2 | 70 | S3 needs 70, W3 has 80; S3 exhausted. W3 remaining: 10 |
| 2 | W1→S2 | $2 | 55 | S2 needs 55, W1 has 90; S2 exhausted. W1 remaining: 35 |
| 3 | W4→S4 | $2 | 50 | S4 needs 50, W4 has 60; S4 exhausted. W4 remaining: 10 |
| 4 | W3→S5 | $3 | 10 | S5 needs 65, W3 has 10; W3 exhausted. S5 remaining: 55 |
| 5 | W1→S1 | $3 | 35 | S1 needs 60, W1 has 35; W1 exhausted. S1 remaining: 25 |
| 6 | W2→S1 | $6 | 25 | S1 needs 25, W2 has 70; S1 exhausted. W2 remaining: 45 |
| 7 | W2→S5 | $7 | 45 | S5 needs 55, W2 has 45; W2 exhausted. S5 remaining: 10 |
| 8 | W4→S5 | $9 | 10 | Last cell; both exhausted. |

**Initial BFS cost:** 70×2 + 55×2 + 50×2 + 10×3 + 35×3 + 25×6 + 45×7 + 10×9  
= 140 + 110 + 100 + 30 + 105 + 150 + 315 + 90 = **$1,040**

### Step 3 — Optimality Check via MODI (UV method)

Set u₁=0. Solve uᵢ+vⱼ=cᵢⱼ for all 8 basic cells:

| | v₁=3 | v₂=2 | v₃=3 | v₄=−3 | v₅=4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| u₁=0 | **W1-S1** 3 | **W1-S2** 2 | — | — | — |
| u₂=3 | **W2-S1** 6 | — | — | — | **W2-S5** 7 |
| u₃=−1 | — | — | **W3-S3** 2 | — | **W3-S5** 3 |
| u₄=5 | — | — | — | **W4-S4** 2 | **W4-S5** 9 |

Reduced cost dᵢⱼ = cᵢⱼ − uᵢ − vⱼ for non-basic cells (negative = improvable):

- W2-S3: 3−3−3 = **−3** ← most negative
- W4-S2: 5−5−2 = **−2**
- W4-S3: 6−5−3 = **−2**
- W2-S2: 4−3−2 = **−1**

### Final MODI Check (Optimality Confirmed)

Recompute dual variables: u₁=0, u₂=2, u₃=1, u₄=3; v₁=3, v₂=2, v₃=1, v₄=−1, v₅=2

All 12 non-basic reduced costs:
