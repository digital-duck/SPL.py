# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A furniture workshop produces chairs and tables. Each chair requires 2 hours of labor and 3 kg of wood, earning $20 profit. Each table requires 4 hours of labor and 5 kg of wood, earning $30 profit. The workshop has 24 hours of labor and 30 kg of wood available per day. Both quantities must be non-negative. Maximize daily profit.

**Solver status:** `Optimal`
**Optimal objective value:** 200

**Decision variables:**
  - chairs = 10
  - tables = 0

## Interpretation

The optimal plan is to produce **10 chairs and 0 tables** per day, yielding a maximum daily profit of **$200**.

Of the two resource constraints, **wood is the binding constraint**: all 30 kg are consumed exactly. Labor is not binding — only 20 of the 24 available hours are used, leaving 4 hours idle.

**Recommendation:** Focus today's production entirely on chairs; the workshop's wood supply is the true bottleneck, and chairs extract more profit per kilogram of wood ($6.67/kg) than tables ($6.00/kg).

---

## Solution Verification

**Substitute chairs = 10, tables = 0 into each constraint:**

**Labor constraint** (limit: 24 hours)
> 2 × 10 + 4 × 0 = 20 + 0 = **20 ≤ 24** ✓
> Slack = 24 − 20 = 4 hours → **not binding**

**Wood constraint** (limit: 30 kg)
> 3 × 10 + 5 × 0 = 30 + 0 = **30 ≤ 30** ✓
> Slack = 30 − 30 = 0 kg → **binding (tight)**

**Non-negativity**
> chairs = 10 ≥ 0 ✓
> tables = 0 ≥ 0 ✓

**Objective calculation:**
> 10 × $20 + 0 × $30 = $200 + $0 = **$200**

This matches the solver's reported value of **200.0** exactly. The solution is verified correct.
