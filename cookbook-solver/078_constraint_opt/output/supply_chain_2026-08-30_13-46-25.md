# Supply Chain Optimization Report

**Problem:** A company has two warehouses and three retail stores. Warehouse W1 has 80 units of inventory; Warehouse W2 has 60 units. Store S1 needs 50 units, Store S2 needs 40 units, Store S3 needs 50 units. Shipping costs per unit: W1 to S1 costs $2, W1 to S2 costs $3, W1 to S3 costs $1; W2 to S1 costs $5, W2 to S2 costs $4, W2 to S3 costs $8. All store demand must be met exactly. Minimize total shipping cost.

**2. Minimum Cost Method (Northwest Corner + MODI):**

The Minimum Cost Method involves starting with the Northwest corner and iteratively improving the solution until no further cost reduction is possible.

* **Step 1: Initial Solution (Northwest Corner)**
   - We start by shipping from W1 to S3: 50 units at $1/unit = $50

* **Step 2: MODI (Least Cost Method)**  Since the Northwest corner solution isn't optimal, we apply the MODI method to find improvements. We iterate through the unfulfilled demands and remaining supplies until all constraints are met.

       - Ship 40 from W2 to S2: Cost = 40 * $4 = $160.
      Total cost so far: $410 + $160 = $570

       - Ship 50 from W1 to S3: Cost = 50 * $1 = $50.
      Total cost so far: $570 + $50 = $620
