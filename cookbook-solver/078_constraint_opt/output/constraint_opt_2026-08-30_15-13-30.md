# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A furniture workshop produces chairs and tables. Each chair requires 2 hours of labor and 3 kg of wood, earning $20 profit. Each table requires 4 hours of labor and 5 kg of wood, earning $30 profit. The workshop has 24 hours of labor and 30 kg of wood available per day. Both quantities must be non-negative. Maximize daily profit.

**Solver status:** `Optimal`
**Optimal objective value:** 200

**Decision variables:**
  - chairs = 10
  - tables = 0

## Interpretation

The optimal decision is to produce **10 chairs and 0 tables** per day. This yields a maximum daily profit of **$200**.

The **wood constraint is binding**: the workshop uses exactly all 30 kg of available wood. The **labor constraint is not binding**: only 20 of the 24 available labor hours are used, leaving 4 hours idle.

Despite tables earning more per unit ($30 vs. $20), chairs are more wood-efficient — they generate $6.67 of profit per kg of wood versus $6.00 for tables — so the workshop maximizes profit by dedicating all wood to chairs.

**Recommendation:** Focus daily production entirely on chairs; the binding constraint is wood supply, so any negotiated increase in wood availability will directly grow profit.

## Solution Verification

**Given:** chairs = 10, tables = 0

**Labor constraint** (2 × chairs + 4 × tables ≤ 24):
2 × 10 + 4 × 0 = 20 + 0 = **20 ≤ 24** ✓
Slack = 24 − 20 = 4 hours → **not binding**

**Wood constraint** (3 × chairs + 5 × tables ≤ 30):
3 × 10 + 5 × 0 = 30 + 0 = **30 ≤ 30** ✓
Slack = 30 − 30 = 0 kg → **binding (tight)**

**Non-negativity** (chairs ≥ 0, tables ≥ 0):
10 ≥ 0 ✓, 0 ≥ 0 ✓ → both satisfied

**Objective value** (20 × chairs + 30 × tables):
20 × 10 + 30 × 0 = 200 + 0 = **$200**

The computed objective **matches** the solver's reported value of $200.00. All constraints are satisfied, and the solution is confirmed correct.
