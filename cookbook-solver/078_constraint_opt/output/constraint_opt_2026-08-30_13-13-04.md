# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A furniture workshop produces chairs and tables. Each chair requires 2 hours of labor and 3 kg of wood, earning $20 profit. Each table requires 4 hours of labor and 5 kg of wood, earning $30 profit. The workshop has 24 hours of labor and 30 kg of wood available per day. Both quantities must be non-negative. Maximize daily profit.

## Solution

### 1. Decision Variables, Objective, and Constraints

Let **x** = chairs produced per day, **y** = tables produced per day.

**Objective:** Maximize Z = 20x + 30y

**Subject to:**
- Labor: 2x + 4y ≤ 24
- Wood:  3x + 5y ≤ 30
- x ≥ 0,  y ≥ 0

---

### 2. Feasible Region Corners

The feasible region is the polygon satisfying all four inequalities. Its corners occur where pairs of boundary lines intersect.

**Corner A — Origin:** (x, y) = (0, 0). Trivially feasible.

**Corner B — Labor boundary meets x-axis (y = 0):**
2x = 24 → x = 12.
Check wood: 3(12) + 5(0) = 36 > 30. **Infeasible** — eliminated.

**Corner C — Wood boundary meets x-axis (y = 0):**
3x = 30 → x = 10.
Check labor: 2(10) + 4(0) = 20 ≤ 24. **Feasible.** → **(10, 0)**

**Corner D — Labor boundary meets y-axis (x = 0):**
4y = 24 → y = 6.
Check wood: 3(0) + 5(6) = 30 ≤ 30. **Feasible.** → **(0, 6)**

**Corner E — Wood boundary meets y-axis (x = 0):**
5y = 30 → y = 6. Same point as Corner D: **(0, 6)**.

**Corner F — Intersection of the two constraint lines:**
Solve the 2×2 system:
```
2x + 4y = 24   ... (i)
3x + 5y = 30   ... (ii)
```
From (i): x = (24 − 4y) / 2 = 12 − 2y.
Substitute into (ii): 3(12 − 2y) + 5y = 30 → 36 − 6y + 5y = 30 → y = 6.
Then x = 12 − 2(6) = 0.

Intersection is **(0, 6)** — the same point as D and E. Both constraints are simultaneously binding there. This is a **degenerate vertex**: the two constraint lines happen to cross exactly on the y-axis.

**Feasible corner vertices:** (0, 0), (10, 0), (0, 6).

---

### 3. Objective Evaluated at Each Corner

| Vertex | Z = 20x + 30y | Calculation |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| (0, 0) | **$0** | 20(0) + 30(0) = 0 |
| (10, 0)| **$200** | 20(10) + 30(0) = 200 |
| (0, 6) | **$180** | 20(0) + 30(6) = 180 |

---

### 4. Optimal Solution

Maximum profit occurs at **(x, y) = (10, 0)**:
produce **10 chairs and 0 tables** for a daily profit of **$200**.

Since chairs and tables are discrete physical units, the optimal LP vertex is already integer-valued — no rounding or branch-and-bound is needed.

---

## Verification

**Plug (x = 10, y = 0) into every constraint:**

**Labor constraint:** 2(10) + 4(0) = 20 + 0 = **20 ≤ 24** ✓  
→ 4 hours of slack (non-binding).

**Wood constraint:** 3(10) + 5(0) = 30 + 0 = **30 ≤ 30** ✓  
→ 0 kg of slack (binding — all 30 kg consumed).

**Non-negativity:** x = 10 ≥ 0 ✓, y = 0 ≥ 0 ✓

**Objective value:** 10 × $20 + 0 × $30 = **$200 + $0 = $200**.

All constraints satisfied, objective confirmed at **$200/day**.
