# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A furniture workshop produces chairs and tables. Each chair requires 2 hours of labor and 3 kg of wood, earning $20 profit. Each table requires 4 hours of labor and 5 kg of wood, earning $30 profit. The workshop has 24 hours of labor and 30 kg of wood available per day. Both quantities must be non-negative. Maximize daily profit.

## Solution

Let:
*   `x` be the number of chairs produced daily.
*   `y` be the number of tables produced daily.

**1. Define Decision Variables and State the Objective and Constraints:**

The objective is to maximize daily profit. The objective function is:

Maximize  Profit = 20x + 30y

Subject to the following constraints:

*   Labor constraint: 2x + 4y ≤ 24
*   Wood constraint: 3x + 5y ≤ 30
*   Non-negativity constraints: x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0

**2. Find the Feasible Region Corners:**

To find the corners of the feasible region, we need to solve the systems of equations formed by the binding constraints.

*   **Constraint 1 (Labor): 2x + 4y = 24**
    Divide by 2: x + 2y = 12  => x = 12 - 2y

*   **Constraint 2 (Wood): 3x + 5y = 30**
    Substitute x = 12 - 2y into the wood constraint:
    3(12 - 2y) + 5y = 30
    36 - 6y + 5y = 30
    -y = 30 - 36
    -y = -6
    y = 6

    Now, solve for x:
    x = 12 - 2(6) = 12 - 12 = 0

    So one corner point is (0, 6).

*   **Corner at the Origin:**  (0, 0) – This is a corner because x and y must be greater than or equal to zero.

*   **Intersection of Labor and Wood Constraints:**
    We have x = 12 - 2y and 3x + 5y = 30. Substitute the first equation into the second:
     3(12-2y) + 5y = 30
     36 - 6y + 5y = 30
     36 - y = 30
    y = 36 - 30 = 6
    x = 12 - 2(6) = 12–12=0.  We already found that point.

*   **Intersection of Labor constraint with the x-axis (x = 0):** If x = 0, then from 2x + 4y = 24 we have 4y = 24, so y = 6. This gives us the point (0, 6) which is an intersection of labor constraint and wood constraint.

*   **Intersection of Wood Constraint with the x-axis (y=0):**  If y = 0, then from 3x + 5y = 30 we have 3x = 30, so x = 10. This gives us the point (10, 0).

*   **Intersection of Labor and Wood Constraints with the x-axis & y axis:**
    As determined above:
    (0,6) - from labor constraint
    (10,0) -  from wood constraint


**3. Evaluate the Objective at Every Corner Vertex:**

Now we evaluate the profit function (20x + 30y) at each corner point:

*   (0, 0): Profit = 20(0) + 30(0) = $0
*   (10, 0): Profit = 20(10) + 30(0) = $200
*   (0, 6): Profit = 20(0) + 30(6) = $180
*   Intersection of Labor and Wood Constraints: (0,6), as previously calculated.  Let's calculate the profit at this point for verification.

To find the intersection accurately, we can solve the system:
x+2y=12 and 3x+5y =30; Multiply first equation by -3 to eliminate x:
-3x-6y=-36 and 3x+5y=30; Adding these gives -y = -6, so y=6. Substituting into the first equation we have x+2(6)=12 or x + 12 = 12 which means x=0.  This is (0,6)
