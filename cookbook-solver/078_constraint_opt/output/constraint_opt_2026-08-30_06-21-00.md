# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A factory makes chairs (2h labor, 4kg wood, $20 profit) and tables (4h labor, 3kg wood, $30 profit). Available: 20h labor, 24kg wood. Maximize profit.

## Interpretation

The solver recommends producing **3.6 chairs and 3.2 tables**, yielding a maximum profit of **$168**. Both resources are fully consumed: all 20 hours of labor and all 24 kg of wood are used, meaning both constraints are **binding**. There is no slack in either resource — the factory is operating at its absolute capacity limit on both dimensions simultaneously. Because the solution contains fractional units, a practical implementation would require rounding to integer quantities (an integer program), which may reduce profit slightly. **Recommendation:** run this as a mixed-integer program to find the best whole-unit production plan near this optimum.

**Wood constraint** (limit: 24 kg)

> = **24.0 kg ≤ 24 kg** ✓ — **binding** (no slack)

**Non-negativity**

> chairs = 3.6 ≥ 0 ✓
> tables = 3.2 ≥ 0 ✓

**Objective value**

> 3.6 × $20 + 3.2 × $30
> = $72 + $96
> = **$168**

The computed value **$168 matches** the solver's reported objective exactly. Both constraints are tight, confirming the solution sits at a corner of the feasible region where labor and wood are simultaneously exhausted.
