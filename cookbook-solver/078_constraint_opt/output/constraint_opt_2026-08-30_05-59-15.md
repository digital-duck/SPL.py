# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A factory makes chairs (2h labor, 4kg wood, $20 profit) and tables (4h labor, 3kg wood, $30 profit). Available: 20h labor, 24kg wood. Maximize profit.

**Solver status:** `Optimal`
**Optimal objective value:** 160

**Decision variables:**
  - chairs = 2
  - tables = 4

## Interpretation

The solver recommends producing **2 chairs and 4 tables** per production cycle. This mix yields a maximum profit of **$160**.

Checking the constraints: the labor budget of 20 hours is fully consumed (binding), meaning every available labor hour is put to work — there is no idle capacity. The wood budget of 24 kg is **not** binding; the plan uses only 20 kg, leaving 4 kg of wood unused.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize securing additional labor hours — that is the only active bottleneck; more wood alone cannot increase profit without more labor to go with it.

## Solution Verification

**Variables:** chairs = 2, tables = 4

**Labor constraint** (limit: 20 h)
2 × 2 + 4 × 4 = 4 + 16 = **20 h** ≤ 20 h → **binding** (used exactly)

**Wood constraint** (limit: 24 kg)
4 × 2 + 3 × 4 = 8 + 12 = **20 kg** ≤ 24 kg → **slack** (4 kg unused)

**Objective value**
2 × $20 + 4 × $30 = $40 + $120 = **$160**

The computed objective of **$160** matches the solver's reported value exactly. Both constraints are satisfied; the solution is feasible and verified correct.

prob = pulp.LpProblem("factory_profit", pulp.LpMaximize)

chairs = pulp.LpVariable("chairs", lowBound=0, cat="Integer")
tables = pulp.LpVariable("tables", lowBound=0, cat="Integer")

prob += 20 * chairs + 30 * tables

prob += 2 * chairs + 4 * tables <= 20
prob += 4 * chairs + 3 * tables <= 24

prob.solve(pulp.PULP_CBC_CMD(msg=0))

_result = {
