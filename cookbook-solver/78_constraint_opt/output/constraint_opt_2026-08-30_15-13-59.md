# Constraint Optimization Report

**Problem:** A furniture workshop produces chairs and tables. Each chair requires 2 hours of labor and 3 kg of wood, earning $20 profit. Each table requires 4 hours of labor and 5 kg of wood, earning $30 profit. The workshop has 24 hours of labor and 30 kg of wood available per day. Both quantities must be non-negative. Maximize daily profit.

## Solution

**1. Decision variables, objective, and constraints**

Let x = chairs produced per day, y = tables produced per day.

**Objective:** Maximize Z = 20x + 30y

**Subject to:**
- 2x + 4y ≤ 24 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(labor hours)
- 3x + 5y ≤ 30 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(wood, kg)
- x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0

---

**2. Finding feasible region corners**

There are four boundary lines: x = 0, y = 0, and the two resource constraints. Every corner is an intersection of two boundaries.

**Intersection A — origin:** x = 0, y = 0 → **(0, 0)**
Check labor: 0 ≤ 24 ✓ &nbsp; Check wood: 0 ≤ 30 ✓ &nbsp; **Feasible.**

**Intersection B — labor line meets y-axis (x = 0):**
2(0) + 4y = 24 → y = 6 → candidate **(0, 6)**
Check wood: 3(0) + 5(6) = 30 ≤ 30 ✓ &nbsp; **Feasible.**

**Intersection C — wood line meets y-axis (x = 0):**
3(0) + 5y = 30 → y = 6 → candidate **(0, 6)**
This is the same point as B. Both constraint boundaries pass through (0, 6). The intersection of the two resource constraint lines therefore also lands on the y-axis.

**Intersection D — labor line meets x-axis (y = 0):**
2x + 4(0) = 24 → x = 12 → candidate **(12, 0)**
Check wood: 3(12) + 5(0) = 36 > 30 ✗ &nbsp; **Infeasible — discard.**

**Intersection E — wood line meets x-axis (y = 0):**
3x + 5(0) = 30 → x = 10 → candidate **(10, 0)**
Check labor: 2(10) + 4(0) = 20 ≤ 24 ✓ &nbsp; **Feasible.**

**Intersection F — two resource constraints:**
Solve 2x + 4y = 24 and 3x + 5y = 30 simultaneously.
From the first: x = 12 − 2y.
Substitute: 3(12 − 2y) + 5y = 30 → 36 − 6y + 5y = 30 → y = 6, x = 0.
This is again **(0, 6)** — already counted.

**Feasible corners: (0, 0), (10, 0), (0, 6).**

---

**3. Evaluate objective at each corner**

| Corner | Z = 20x + 30y |
|--------|--------------|
| (0, 0) | 20(0) + 30(0) = **$0** |
| (10, 0) | 20(10) + 30(0) = **$200** |
| (0, 6) | 20(0) + 30(6) = **$180** |

---

**4. Optimal solution**

The maximum is **Z = $200** at **(x, y) = (10, 0)**: produce **10 chairs and 0 tables**.

*Integrality note:* Both values are non-negative integers, so the LP relaxation solution is also a valid integer solution — no rounding is needed.

---

## Verification

Plug x = 10, y = 0 into every constraint:

**Labor:** 2(10) + 4(0) = 20 + 0 = **20 ≤ 24** ✓ &nbsp; Slack = 24 − 20 = 4 hours (not binding).

**Wood:** 3(10) + 5(0) = 30 + 0 = **30 ≤ 30** ✓ &nbsp; Slack = 0 (binding — wood is fully consumed).

**Non-negativity:** x = 10 ≥ 0 ✓, y = 0 ≥ 0 ✓.

**Objective:** 10 × $20 + 0 × $30 = $200 + $0 = **$200**.

The optimal daily profit is **$200**, achieved by making 10 chairs and no tables. Wood is the binding constraint; 4 labor hours remain unused. Chairs dominate tables on both resource ratios ($20/2 hrs = $10/hr vs. $30/4 hrs = $7.50/hr, and $20/3 kg ≈ $6.67/kg vs. $30/5 kg = $6.00/kg), so the optimizer dedicates all available wood to chairs.
