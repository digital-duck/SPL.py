# Supply Chain Optimization Report

**Problem:** A company has two warehouses and three retail stores. Warehouse W1 has 80 units of inventory; Warehouse W2 has 60 units. Store S1 needs 50 units, Store S2 needs 40 units, Store S3 needs 50 units. Shipping costs per unit: W1 to S1 costs $2, W1 to S2 costs $3, W1 to S3 costs $1; W2 to S1 costs $5, W2 to S2 costs $4, W2 to S3 costs $8. All store demand must be met exactly. Minimize total shipping cost.

## Solution

### Step 1 — Cost matrix and supply/demand table

```
          S1    S2    S3  | Supply
  W1      $2    $3    $1  |   80
  W2      $5    $4    $8  |   60
  -----+-----+-----+-----+
Demand    50    40    50  |  140
```

Total supply = 80 + 60 = 140. Total demand = 50 + 40 + 50 = 140. The problem is **balanced** — no dummy node needed.

Required basic variables: m + n − 1 = 2 + 3 − 1 = **4**.

---

### Step 2 — Initial feasible solution via Minimum Cost Method

Repeatedly allocate to the cheapest available cell.

**Iteration 1.** Cheapest cell: W1→S3 at $1.  
Allocate min(80, 50) = **50 units**.  
W1 remaining: 80 − 50 = 30. S3 satisfied (close column).

**Iteration 2.** Remaining cells (S3 excluded). Cheapest: W1→S1 at $2.  
Allocate min(30, 50) = **30 units**.  
W1 remaining: 30 − 30 = 0 (W1 exhausted; close row). S1 remaining: 50 − 30 = 20.

**Iteration 3.** Only W2 (60 units) remains. Open demands: S1 = 20, S2 = 40.  
Cheapest available: W2→S1 at $5. Allocate **20 units**.  
S1 satisfied. W2 remaining: 60 − 20 = 40.

**Iteration 4.** W2→S2 at $4. Allocate **40 units**.  
W2 exhausted. S2 satisfied.

Initial allocation:

| Route  | Units | Unit cost |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| W1→S1  |  30   |  $2       |
| W1→S3  |  50   |  $1       |
| W2→S1  |  20   |  $5       |
| W2→S2  |  40   |  $4       |

Initial cost = 30×2 + 50×1 + 20×5 + 40×4 = 60 + 50 + 100 + 160 = **$370**.

---

### Step 3 — Optimality check via MODI method

Assign dual variables u_i (rows) and v_j (columns) using the rule **u_i + v_j = c_ij** for every basic cell. Set u₁ = 0.

| Basic cell | Equation           | Result  |
|------------|-------------------|---------|
| W1→S3      | u₁ + v₃ = 1      | v₃ = 1  |
| W1→S1      | u₁ + v₁ = 2      | v₁ = 2  |
| W2→S1      | u₂ + v₁ = 5      | u₂ = 3  |
| W2→S2      | u₂ + v₂ = 4      | v₂ = 1  |

Compute the **reduced cost** d_ij = c_ij − u_i − v_j for each non-basic cell:

| Non-basic cell | c_ij | u_i | v_j | d_ij        |
|----------------|------|-----|-----|-------------|
| W1→S2          |  $3  |  0  |  1  | 3−0−1 = **+2** |
| W2→S3          |  $8  |  3  |  1  | 8−3−1 = **+4** |

Both reduced costs are strictly positive. **No improving direction exists.** The initial solution is already optimal.

### Optimal shipping plan

- **W1 → S1: 30 units**
- **W1 → S3: 50 units**
- **W2 → S1: 20 units**
- **W2 → S2: 40 units**

**Total minimum cost: $370**

## Verification

### Supply constraints

| Warehouse | Shipped         | Arithmetic      | Supply | Status   |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|--------|----------|
| W1        | S1 + S3         | 30 + 50 = **80**| 80     | Binding  |
| W2        | S1 + S2         | 20 + 40 = **60**| 60     | Binding  |

Both warehouses fully used; zero slack.

### Demand constraints

| Store | Received        | Arithmetic        | Demand | Status   |
|-------|-----------------|-------------------|--------|----------|
| S1    | W1 + W2         | 30 + 20 = **50**  | 50     | Exactly met |
| S2    | W2 only         | 40 = **40**       | 40     | Exactly met |
| S3    | W1 only         | 50 = **50**       | 50     | Exactly met |

### Total cost breakdown

```
W1→S1:  30 × $2  =  $60
W1→S3:  50 × $1  =  $50
W2→S1:  20 × $5  = $100
W2→S2:  40 × $4  = $160
                  ------
Total             = $370
```

The optimal plan ships **140 units** across four routes at a total cost of **$370**, with all supply exhausted and all demand exactly satisfied.
